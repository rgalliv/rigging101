The rigging system

One hook.
One load path.

A rigged load is a single connected load path -- every component between the hook and the load carries the lift, and the weakest or most misapplied link governs the whole assembly. Capacity depends on the hitch, included angle, hardware, bearing surfaces, and center of gravity, all working within the marked capacity on the sling tag and hardware.
Orientation only. This guide does not replace the sling identification tag, manufacturer capacity data, applicable inspection criteria, lift plan, or direction from a qualified person. Actual capacities vary by manufacturer, construction, grade, configuration, and condition.
