## Classroom source status

Every new independent packet is authored educational content. Shipping records, tag IDs, capacities, section-loss limits, wind hold points and beam configurations are fictional. These records demonstrate how to use controlling data; they do not replace real product tags or inspection records. The spreader table intentionally uses no manufacturer name or real equipment model.

Inherited IPT wire-rope diagrams are now unscored diagnostic study material. Their precise edition and reproduction permissions still require a publisher/source-owner review before public release. Existing configuration photographs and reference images were not newly commissioned or independently certified by this revision.

English and Spanish content has automated functional coverage and local editorial review. Technical SME and bilingual SME approval remain human release checks. This revision makes no new certification-equivalence or field-qualification claim.
